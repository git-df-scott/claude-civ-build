## 4. Engine research findings (all verified live, several contradict the wikis)

- `GAME_NO_BARBARIANS` / `GAME_HANDICAP` are the real config keys — found via
  `DB.ConfigurationQuery` on the Parameters table; every guessed name was nil.
- **`TECH_HORSEBACK_RIDING` requires TECH_ARCHERY** in this ruleset, not Animal
  Husbandry. The wiki-derived beeline was silently unreachable until checked
  against `GameInfo.TechnologyPrereqs`.
- **Map gave us zero horses** → UNIT_HORSEMAN unbuildable all game. The Saka
  Horse Archer needs no horses and is PROMOTION_CLASS_RANGED → **Agoge** boosts
  it, not Maneuver (Maneuver was slotted early and boosting nothing).
- **Ranged units cannot capture cities**; melee/cavalry required. Rams/Siege
  Towers already unlocked via Masonry/Construction when needed.
- POLICY_DISCIPLINE (+5 vs barbarians) is a dead card with barbarians off.
- War API: `UI.RequestPlayerOperation` + `PlayerOperations.DIPLOMACY_DECLARE_WAR`
  / `DIPLOMACY_MAKE_PEACE`; policies via `Culture:RequestEnactPolicy` +
  `CanSlotPolicy`; both verified by state change (`IsAtWarWith`, `IsPolicyActive`).

## 5. Mistakes made and lessons banked (the "learn from all mistakes" ledger)

**Strategic**
1. **No defensive doctrine** — the t160s collapse. All force committed to one
   siege; p1 walked into three open cities. (Previous campaign died the same
   way; the lesson was in my notes and I still rebuilt without it.)
2. **No economic feedback** — treasury hit 0 and the army auto-disbanded while
   "winning". Inverse of last campaign's gold-hoarding, same root: economy
   wasn't part of the policy at all.
3. Both now fixed structurally: regroup/break-off doctrine, peace-seeking when
   weak, walls+defenders under invasion, broke-mode economy — all with
   **hysteresis** (every single-threshold behaviour oscillated until given
   separate enter/exit points).

**The dominant bug class — "accepted, did nothing":** MOVE_TO into fog;
MOVE_TO onto a city tile without the ATTACK modifier; all units funnelling to
one adjacent tile (1 attack/turn from 16 units); production caps silently
defeated by a fallback that re-added what they removed (19 builders, 7
simultaneous rams); a purchase check that logged 2g of income drift as a 150g
buy; ring-spread scattering the rally it was meant to concentrate;
**FinishIdle cancelling every multi-turn path** (army frozen 14 turns while
logging moved=14). Every one of these logged success. The fix in every case:
verify by state change — positions, treasury delta ≥ unit cost, IsAtWarWith,
build-queue hash — never by the ack.

**Process**
- `TaskStop` kills the task wrapper but **leaves the python process alive** —
  two runners drove the game concurrently for ~40 min issuing conflicting
  orders. Now: kill by command-line match before every relaunch.
- PowerShell 5.1 `Set-Content -Encoding utf8` writes a BOM → crashed the
  runner's JSON state. Repaired with `utf-8-sig`.

**Still open** (flagged, not hidden): army concentrates only partially
(massing 1–2/8 at report time); builder improvements never execute
(`built=0` throughout — the 19→4 builder decline was p1 killing them, not work).
